Questions

How do I listen to GlowWave FM?

Press the play button on this page. The station streams live in your browser, free, with no sign-up.

Does GlowWave FM broadcast 24/7?

Yes. GlowWave FM streams live around the clock as part of the Glow Network.

Is there a version for my phone or car?

Yes. The Glow Network has dedicated players for iPhone, Android Auto, and a lighter Chill stream, alongside the main player and app.

Does the Glow Network have IPTV too?

Yes. GlowTV is the Glow Network's IPTV streaming service, available at glowtv.shop/glowtv, with live channels across sports, news, movies and entertainment.

What is GlowTV?

GlowTV is the Glow Network's IPTV service: live TV streamed online to your phone, computer, smart TV or IPTV box, without a cable subscription.

Do I need a cable subscription for GlowTV?

No. GlowTV streams live TV over the internet, so no cable subscription is required.
