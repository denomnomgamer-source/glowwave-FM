About GlowWave FM

GlowWave FM is a free internet radio station from the Glow Network. It streams live around the clock, works in any modern browser and needs no sign-up or app.

Made for listening

Live audio visualizer

Watch the music move. Switch between bars, a waveform line and a glowing ring, all drawn from the live audio.

Tab icon that moves

While you listen, the browser tab icon turns into a tiny live visualizer, so you can see GlowWave is on from any tab.

Reliable stream

If the main stream drops, the player switches to a backup automatically. Use the space bar to play or pause.

Questions

How do I listen to Glowwave FM?

Press the play button on this page. The station streams live in your browser on desktop and mobile.

Does it cost anything?

No. Glowwave FM is free to listen to.

Why is there no sound after pressing play?

Check your device volume and the volume slider on the player. Browsers only start audio after you press play.
