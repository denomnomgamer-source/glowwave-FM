Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

01 Our Commitment to Privacy

At Glowwave-FM, your privacy is paramount. We believe in providing a seamless, high-quality internet radio and media experience without compromising your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data on our platform.

02 We Do Not Store Your Stuff

We do not collect, store, or process any personal user data. When you tune into our live stream or browse our website:

  • We do not require user accounts or registration.
  • We do not track or store personal listening history tied to identifiable individuals.
  • We do not use invasive cookies or tracking pixels to profile our visitors.
  • Any inquiries sent via email are strictly used for correspondence and are never sold or shared with third parties.

03 Third-Party Streaming & Links

Our audio stream is delivered via secure standard protocols. External links (such as IPTV services or partner networks) are governed by their respective privacy policies. We encourage you to review policies on external websites if you choose to navigate away from Glowwave-FM.
